# Licensing policy

## Scope

GGEN-owned source and documentation intentionally distributed in this repository are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE), with attribution in [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE).

Apache-2.0 does not automatically apply to:

- third-party dependencies, SDKs, models, fonts or generated data;
- personal signatures, photographs or user documents;
- government or institutional logos, seals, borders or watermarks;
- production templates or OCR/model files supplied through the private asset vault;
- trademarks, product names, logos or other brand identity.

A public repository does not create permission to redistribute material merely because a hash, filename or abstract ID is visible here. Unknown provenance or unknown distribution rights block a release.

## Intake and release rule

Every dependency, asset and model requires a provenance record before admission. The record must identify an immutable source URL, version or commit, SHA-256, size, SPDX license identifier or an explicitly documented pending state, copyright owner, notice/license text, modification state and distribution permission. See [`dependency-intake.md`](dependency-intake.md), [`asset-and-model-intake.md`](asset-and-model-intake.md), and [`sbom-and-provenance.md`](sbom-and-provenance.md).

Commercial and Google Play release eligibility is a separate gate. An item may be present in a private development environment while remaining blocked from public artifacts. The release policies are [`commercial-release-policy.md`](commercial-release-policy.md) and [`google-play-release-policy.md`](google-play-release-policy.md).

## Contributions

Contributions must follow the DCO-style sign-off rule in [`contributor-ip-policy.md`](contributor-ip-policy.md). Contributors must not submit material they do not have the right to license, and must identify third-party content rather than asserting ownership by default.

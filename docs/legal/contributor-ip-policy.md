# Contributor intellectual-property policy

GGEN uses a DCO-style contribution rule in addition to the Apache-2.0 license.

By submitting a contribution, the contributor represents that they have the right to submit it and agree to license their contribution under Apache-2.0 as part of GGEN, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. The contributor must add a sign-off line to each commit:

```text
Signed-off-by: Full Name <authoritative-email@example.com>
```

The sign-off must identify the contributor; it is not a claim that the contributor owns every dependency or reference mentioned by the change. Contributions containing third-party code, data, fonts, models, images, signatures, logos, seals, templates or generated material must include an intake record and license/permission evidence. If evidence is unavailable, do not submit the material.

Do not submit confidential information, personal data, credentials, private asset bytes, or content copied from BG/RGEN or another project without a documented license and technical boundary. Maintainers may reject or remove a contribution that cannot pass provenance, security, privacy or public-safety review.

The contributor retains copyright in their contribution, while granting the project the Apache-2.0 rights described above. This policy does not grant trademark rights or permission to use protected asset packs.

# Contributing to GGEN

Read [`AI_ASSISTANT.md`](AI_ASSISTANT.md), [`docs/legal/licensing-policy.md`](docs/legal/licensing-policy.md), and [`docs/legal/contributor-ip-policy.md`](docs/legal/contributor-ip-policy.md) before contributing.

GGEN prefers small, complete, documented, tested platform-neutral foundations over demo-quality buttons. Computer-quality semantics and mobile-friendly interaction are both mandatory. Do not copy BG/RGEN or competitor UI, branding or implementation. Do not modify or submit private protected asset bytes.

## DCO-style sign-off

Every commit submitted for review must include:

```text
Signed-off-by: Full Name <authoritative-email@example.com>
```

The sign-off certifies that the contributor has the right to submit the change and agrees that the contribution may be licensed under Apache-2.0 as part of GGEN. It does not certify third-party ownership or grant trademark rights. Third-party code, data, fonts, models, images, signatures, logos, seals and templates require provenance and permission receipts before submission.

## Review expectations

Run the relevant dependency-free governance checks and package tests. Update documentation and provenance when behavior, claims, dependencies or distribution boundaries change. Keep credentials, personal data, user documents, private references and build artifacts out of Git.
